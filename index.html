★Our promise to families

Color World Kids is a coloring game for young children made by RahiVerse Studio (“we”, “us”). We built it to be safe for little ones: the game works completely offline and does not collect, share or sell any personal information.

✎What the game keeps on your device

To make the game fun and personal, a few things are saved only on the phone or tablet where the game is installed. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

WhatWhyWhere it is kept
Child’s first name (up to 5 letters, typed by the family) and a cartoon avatar To greet the child (“Great job, name!”) and show the name on their pictures and certificates Only on the device
Paintings, stars and level progress So the child can continue where they left off Only on the device
Settings (music, sound effects, vibration, volume) To remember the family’s choices Only on the device

We recommend using only a first name or a nickname. Nothing typed into the game ever leaves the device.

✓What we do NOT do

  • We do not ask for or collect email addresses, phone numbers, locations, photos, contacts or device IDs.
  • The game does not use the internet: it has no internet permission, no accounts, no log-in and no online chat.
  • There are no advertisements, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not use the camera or microphone.
  • We never sell, rent or share any information with anyone.

⚙Permissions the game uses

  • Vibration – for gentle, playful vibration when buttons are tapped. It can be switched off in Settings.
  • Storage (only on Android 9 and older) – used only when the family taps Save, to put the child’s picture or certificate into the device’s photo gallery. On newer Android versions no permission is needed.

Talking voice: the game uses the device’s own built-in text-to-speech engine to say short phrases such as “Great job!”. This runs on the device; the game does not send any text to us. The text-to-speech engine is part of the device and follows its provider’s own policy.

♥Children’s privacy

Color World Kids is designed for children and follows the principles of children’s privacy laws such as the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR, and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as well as the Google Play Families Policy. Because the game collects no personal information, no data about children is ever gathered, stored on our servers or shared.

Parent area: sending feedback by email and opening this privacy policy are placed behind a grown-ups-only question (a small math puzzle), so children cannot leave the game by accident.

⌫Deleting the saved information

Families are always in control:

  • Open Parents → Reset Game to delete the name, avatar, paintings, stars and progress at once.
  • Or simply uninstall the game: everything it saved is removed from the device.
  • Pictures you chose to Save to the gallery stay in the gallery and can be deleted there like any photo.

↻Changes to this policy

If we ever add new features (for example ads or purchases) that change how information is handled, we will update this policy before those features are released, change the “Effective” date above and, where required, ask for a parent’s consent first. We will always follow the Google Play Families Policy.
